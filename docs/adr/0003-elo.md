# ADR 0003: Pairwise Elo for hotness votes

- Status: Accepted for MVP
- Date: 2026-11-01

## Context

Voters can more easily choose between two portraits than assign an absolute 1–10 score. The ranking is explicitly subjective.

## Decision

Use the product name **Hot or Not** for pairwise Elo with initial rating 1500 and K=24. Put newly consented and least-recently shown guest candidates at the head of a fair queue and pair the selected guest with a nearby gallery rating; fall back to gallery-versus-gallery when no eligible guest is waiting. The eligible pool contains only gallery candidates with a rights-cleared portrait and guest candidates with current, explicit Hot or Not consent. Do not show a guest their own candidate. Show a participating guest their current community rank directly, and show all currently consented human players in a separate portrait rail using only their chosen nickname, rank, and Elo. Keep lookalike similarity separate from the preference ranking. Record each matchup and vote as an immutable event; apply rating changes in one database transaction and make each vote idempotent.

## Consequences

Elo produces a relative ordering from observed choices, not an objective attractiveness score. Removing or expiring a consented upload deactivates and anonymizes its guest candidate without erasing historical vote events. The initial version is a single community ranking; per-user rankings can be added later without mixing personal preferences into the community score. Add abuse controls before opening voting broadly.
